Harold

A line that draws itself. Scroll down.

Here are some words, the kind any page has. Harold doesn't loop words, only pictures, so the line wanders past them on its way somewhere.

A picture went by, and the line went round it once and a half, the way you'd frame something with a pencil while you were on the phone.

Scroll back up and it undraws. Scroll down again and it comes back exactly the same: the wobble is seeded from the page's address, so every visitor sees the same line.

Two side by side get one loop each, left first.

And then it walks off down the page.

Harold is one file you can put on any site, or a skill your Claude can add for you: github.com/MsDervish/harold. Made by Kate Tyson in Code for Creatives, from the front page of nomadfarm.
